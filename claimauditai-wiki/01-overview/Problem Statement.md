# Problem Statement

> Healthcare payment integrity systems are plagued by reactive detection pipelines, resulting in massive financial leakage, long adjudication delays, and high false-positive audit rates.

In standard healthcare payment systems, claim audits occur retrospectively in a "pay-and-chase" format. Payers disburse funds first and attempt to recoup fraudulent or upcoded claims 14 to 16 months later. This lag leads to huge losses, as up to 3% to 10% of total healthcare expenditures are lost to fraud, waste, and abuse (FWA).

Furthermore, legacy rule-based engines generate false-positive rates of 30% to 50%, clogging manual audit review queues with low-risk claims. These systems cannot process unstructured progress notes (which contain the clinical truth) at the time of claim submission, allowing upcoded CPT procedures to pass undetected.

## Key Details
- **Financial Leakage**: 3% to 10% of national healthcare spending lost to FWA.
- **Audit Lag**: 14 to 16 months retrospective recovery ("pay-and-chase") delay.
- **False-Positive Rates**: 30% to 50% in traditional deterministic rule engines.
- **Clinical Data Gap**: Legacy engines ignore unstructured clinician narratives at transaction time.
