# YouTube Walkthrough Checklist

> The YouTube Walkthrough Checklist maps out the production sequence for an engaging, high-impact video demonstration of the platform.

Our contest video should be professional, concise, and highlight the platform's key capabilities:

- [ ] **Executive Pitch (0:00 - 0:45)**: Pitch the paradigm shift from reactive "pay-and-chase" to real-time pre-payment prevention.
- [ ] **Architecture Breakdown (0:45 - 2:00)**: Walk through our custom interactions strategy and three-tiered AI engine.
- [ ] **Real-Time Adjudication Demo (2:00 - 3:30)**:
  - Submit clinical narratives (`sample_patient_bundle.json`).
  - Submit the anomalous claim (`sample_claim.json`).
  - Show the returned **`202 Accepted`** response and explainable hold report.
- [ ] **Database Triage Verification (3:30 - 4:30)**: Query the database to show the generated `ClaimResponse`, manual audit `Task`, and `CommunicationRequest` notifications.
- [ ] **Closing Summary (4:30 - 5:00)**: Highlight how our platform leverages native IRIS capabilities to win the contest.

## Key Details
- **Target Duration**: Under 5 minutes.
- **Voice Tone**: Confident, direct, and highly technical.
- **Key Visual Elements**: Side-by-side terminal commands and Management Portal views.
- **Audio Output**: Professional, noise-cancelled vocal track.
